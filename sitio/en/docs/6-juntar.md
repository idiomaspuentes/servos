---
titulo: Bring the team's work together
resumen: Share a book in a .servos file, receive it on another device, and choose between two versions.
---

Each person works on their own device, without internet. So that everyone's work comes together, it is passed in **.servos** files from hand to hand: by memory card, USB, Bluetooth or in a message.

## Share a book

1. In the book, tap **More › Share this book**.
2. Tap **Save the file**. On the phone it is saved where you choose, or sent by Bluetooth or in a message; on the computer, Servos asks where to save it.
3. Pass the file to the other person.

![Share a book](compartir.webp "Everything done in the book goes with it")

**Everything** done in the book goes with it: the text, the alignment, the checks, the study notes, the recordings and the glossary. If the project is **Only the team**, the file carries the password the team agreed on, and its name does not say what it is; whoever receives it needs the password to open it.

To pass the **whole project**, for example when the person who coordinates has just created it, on the home screen tap the project's **Settings** and then **Share the project**: all its books and the glossary go with it.

## Receive someone else's work

1. Open the **.servos** file with Servos: on the phone, tap it and choose Servos; on the computer, double-click it or drag it into the window. You can also go to the book, tap **More › Receive work** and then **Choose the file**.
2. **Before putting anything together**, Servos tells you what the file brings: whose it is, which project it goes to, and how many verses are new, the same, or written differently.
3. Tap **Receive**.

![Before receiving, what it brings](recibir.webp "Servos tells you what it brings and what changes (in the app's example)")
![What came in](recibido.webp "What came in, and what you need to choose")

The new verses come in by themselves. Nothing of yours is deleted.

## When a verse arrives written differently

If two people wrote the same verse differently, Servos does not choose for you: it keeps both versions and the book shows **Verses to bring together**. Tap **Bring together** and, section by section, read the two versions and **keep one**, or go **verse by verse** and make a mix.

![Choose between two versions](juntar.webp "The two versions, to choose from")

## How the team works

In **More › How the team works** you choose between two ways:

- **In parts**: the team shares out chapters, sections or verses. What arrives written another way is chosen, as above.
- **All the same**: each person translates the whole passage. When you receive the others' versions, they are brought together and the best of each one stays.

> **A copy outside the device protects the work** from a phone that breaks or gets lost. Share your books often, even if you work alone, and keep the file on a memory card or a USB stick.
