## The Translation step

In the book, tap the **Translation** step. You do not need to finish the study first: each person can go back to **Study** whenever they want, and the book keeps where they left off in each step.

In the Translation step there are two ways to work: **Written translation**, in the editor, and **Oral translation**, recording each verse. This page is about the editor.

## The editor on the phone

Tap **Start** on **Written translation**. The editor shows one section at a time, with **one box for each verse**. Write in the box; what you write saves by itself, on the device, while you write.

![The verse boxes, empty](editor-vacio.webp "One box for each verse")
![The boxes with the translation](editor-con-texto.webp "A translated section, in the app's example")

At the top are the other columns: **TPL**, **TPS**, the original (**Hebrew** or **Greek**) and **Helps**. On the phone you move from one to another by **sliding your finger** to the left or by tapping its name; the box you were writing in keeps its place. In **Helps** are the notes, the questions and the key words **of the verse you are writing**.

![The bridge text beside it](editor-tpl.webp "The TPL, one finger away from the box")
![The verse's helps](editor-ayudas.webp "The notes of the verse being written")

Move to the next section with **›**, at the bottom.

> The pictures with work done show **the example that comes with the app** ("See the example as Ana"). Its team translates into Kichwa, but the app has no real translation in that language: instead it uses the text of the bridge texts, in Spanish.

## The editor on the computer

In a wide window, each verse is a row: the bridge text on one side, its box on the other, and the helps in a column. You move from one box to the next with the keyboard.

![The editor on a computer](computadora-editor.webp "On the computer, everything in view")

## And also

- **Your study note**, if you wrote one, appears with its verses.
- **Project glossary**, on the home screen or in the book (**More › Project glossary**): the words of the original and how the team agreed to translate them.
- **In the book, More › Save the book as USFM**: the draft in the format that Paratext, translationCore and Door43 read.

> **Each person writes with their own name.** If the device is shared, before writing check at the top who is working; to change, tap your name on the home screen and choose **Change person**.
