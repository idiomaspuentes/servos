---
titulo: Install Servos
resumen: On an Android phone, on a Windows computer, or in the browser without installing anything.
---

Servos is the same app in three places. Choose the one your team has; you can use all three at once and put the work together later.

> **These steps are temporary.** The apps do not have a recognized signature yet, so Android and Windows show a warning before installing them. When they have one, they will install without these extra steps.

The files are in [Download](/#descargar). Not everyone needs to download them from the internet: one person can download them and pass them to the others by cable, memory card, Bluetooth, Xender or nearby sharing. Both apps already carry the Spanish bridge texts and helps inside, and the Hebrew and Greek: you do not need to pass anything else.

## On an Android phone

1. Download **Servos-0.1.0-rc.1.apk** (38 MB), or pass it to the phone, and tap it in the folder where it arrived (Downloads, Bluetooth, Xender or the memory card).
2. If the phone asks, choose **Allow from this source**. Then go back and tap **Install**.
3. A **Play Protect** warning may appear saying that it does not know the app. Tap **More details** and then **Install anyway**: the warning shows because the app does not come from the store.
4. Open Servos and go on with [The first time](/docs/primera-vez/).

With **Xender** or another sharing app, you can pass the installed app from one phone to another, without downloading it again. You can also use it to pass the work files (**.servos**).

> **When you install a new version** over the old one, the work on the phone stays. What you lose, if you **delete** the app, is what you did not share: before you delete it, share your books (see [Bring the work together](/docs/juntar/)).

## On a Windows computer

1. Download **Servos_0.1.0-rc.1_x64-setup.exe** (242 MB) and open it. It is big because it carries everything it needs to install without internet.
2. Windows may say **«Windows protected your PC»**. Click **More info** and then **Run anyway**: the warning shows because the installer is not signed yet.
3. Follow the installer's steps. Servos installs only for the person who uses the computer, without asking for administrator permission.
4. Servos is then in the Start menu. **.servos** files open in Servos with a double click.

## In the browser, without installing

Open [servos.idiomaspuentes.org/app](/app/) in Chrome, Edge or Firefox, once, with internet. Servos is saved in the browser, and from then on it opens without internet at that same address. On a phone, the browser menu offers **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**, so you can open it like the other apps.

In the browser, the texts and helps do not come inside: you receive them once, as [The first time](/docs/primera-vez/#receive-the-texts-and-helps) explains.

> **In the browser**, your work is saved in that browser on that device. If the browser's data is deleted, your work is deleted too. Share your books often, as on any device.
