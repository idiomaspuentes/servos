titulo: Publish on Door43
resumen: Upload the project when there is internet and mark versions, without writing over what others uploaded.
---

Door43 is where translations made with these resources are published. Publishing is the only part of Servos that needs internet; everything else is done without it.

## Link your account, once

The person who publishes needs a Door43 account. You link it only once on each device:

1. Tap your name, on the home screen, and choose **Door43 account**. (You can also get there from the book: the **Publishing** step, **Start**, **Link my Door43 account**.)
2. Write your Door43 username and password, and tap **Link**. The password is used only once, to create a key for that device: **the password is not saved**.

![Publish on Door43](door43.webp "To publish, first you link the account")
![The Door43 account](door43-cuenta.webp "The password is not saved")

If you prefer not to write your password on the device, create a key on Door43 and choose **Use a Door43 key instead of the password**.

## Upload the project

In the **Publishing** step, tap **Start**. Servos tells you, book by book, whether it was already uploaded and whether it changed since then. When you upload, **everything translated** in the project goes, each book with its alignment, to a team repository on Door43, which keeps the history of each upload.

> **What is published on Door43 can be seen by anyone on the internet**, with the name of the account that uploaded it.

**Without internet, nothing is lost.** You can ask for it to be uploaded anyway: the request waits on the device, and when there is internet Servos asks you before sending it.

**What someone else uploaded is not written over.** If someone on the team uploaded changes that are not on your device, Servos does not upload on top of them: it asks you to bring them in and put them together first.

## Mark a version

When the team is happy with what is on Door43, you mark a **numbered version** (v1, v2…). The recordings of the oral translation are attached to the version.
