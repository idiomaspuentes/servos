Door43 is a website where translation teams keep and publish their work. It is free. With an account there, Servos can publish what the team translates and keep a copy of each person's work. It is the only part of Servos that needs internet; everything else is done without it.

## Your account, once

Each person who publishes or keeps their copy on Door43 needs an account. You link it only once on each device:

1. Tap your name, on the home screen, and choose **Door43 account**.
2. If you do not have an account yet, tap **Create a Door43 account ›**: Door43 opens in the browser. When you have your account, come back to Servos.
3. Write your Door43 username and password, and tap **Link**. The password is used only once, to create a key for that device: **the password is not saved**.

![Publish on Door43](door43.webp "To publish, first you link the account")
![The Door43 account](door43-cuenta.webp "The password is not saved")

If you prefer not to write your password on the device, create a key on Door43 (in **Settings › Applications**, with permission to write repositories) and choose **Use a Door43 key instead of the password**.

## The team's organization

An organization is the team's place on Door43: its projects are kept there, and only those who are in it can save to them. You choose it once, the first time you publish or leave your work on Door43. Servos shows the organizations your account is already in and offers two more ways:

- **Create one for the team**: the person who coordinates does this. Choose the name and **who can see what it keeps**: **Only its members** or **Anyone**. You are in it and can add the others.
- **Find my team's**: write its name and choose it. Door43 has no way to ask to join an organization: the person who manages it has to add you. Tap **Copy my user name** and send it to them; the person who coordinates adds you from Servos, in the book, **More › The team's work on Door43**.

Organizations that only their members can see do not show up when you search. If you cannot find your team's, ask the person who coordinates for its name.

## The copy of your work

If the phone is lost or breaks, your work is also on Door43, and the team brings it in from **The team's work on Door43**. The copy goes by itself, when there is internet: a few minutes after a change, never while you are writing.

In **Door43 account › A copy of your work** you see the last copy and you choose:

- **Keep a copy on Door43 when there is internet**, on or off. In projects for **Anyone who has the file** it comes on. In projects for **Only the team** you turn it on by hand, and the copy goes sealed with the team's password: only the team can open it.
- **On which internet**: **Only on Wi-Fi**, as it comes, or **Mobile data too**.

While the copy works, Servos says nothing. It only warns you on the home screen if for days the copy could not be kept, and it says why.

> **On the phone**, Servos talks to Door43 only if the network shows it Door43's certificate. On a network that shows another one, it sends nothing and says so: your work stays on the phone.

## The team's work on Door43

In the book, **More › The team's work on Door43** is for passing the work to each other over the internet, the same as with a file by card or Bluetooth:

- **Leave my work on Door43**: leaves yours.
- **Bring in the team's work**: brings in what the others left and puts it together verse by verse, as in [Bring the work together](/docs/juntar/).

## Upload the project

In the book, tap the **Publishing** step and, on **Publish on Door43**, tap **Start**. The first time, Servos asks in which organization the team keeps its work. Then it tells you, book by book, whether it was already uploaded and whether it changed since then. When you upload, **everything translated** in the project goes, each book with its alignment, to a team repository on Door43, which keeps the history of each upload.

> **What is published on Door43 can be seen by anyone on the internet** if the organization is for **Anyone**, with the name of the account that uploaded it.

**Without internet, nothing is lost.** You can ask for it to be uploaded anyway: the request waits on the device, the home screen reminds you, and when there is internet Servos asks you before sending it.

**What someone else uploaded is not written over.** If someone on the team uploaded changes that are not on your device, Servos does not upload on top of them: it asks you to bring them in and put them together first.

## Mark a version

When the team is happy with what is on Door43, you mark a **numbered version** (v1, v2…). The recordings of the oral translation are attached to the version.
